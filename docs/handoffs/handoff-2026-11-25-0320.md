## 5. Key Decisions & Rationale

- **Split the plan into A (additive) and B (destructive), seam after WU-4.** Not arbitrary: the
  design already required every destructive change to come *after* WU-4 (so WU-4 could be
  demoable). A is independently valuable and cheap to abandon; B changes existing deployments.
- **Plan size was the review blocker.** One 1620-line plan got **zero gate verdicts in three
  attempts** (600 s wall clock twice, provider idle-stream timeout once; 15 spawns). Splitting
  is the fix; do not merge them back.
- **The pin is read inside `cachedSessionExec`, not passed in.** Three designs were tried:
  (1) a `securityConfigForTarget` bridge — **rejected: unsafe**, it bypassed `mode` and moved
  untrusted execution onto the harness box; (2) threading `Maybe MachineName` — **rejected**,
  16+3 call sites, two inside the modules being changed; (3) read `session.json` inside the
  cache via `readSessionMeta` — **adopted**, `cachedSessionExec`'s type unchanged.
- **C9 — a deliberate, documented deviation from D9.** A persists the pin **request**, not its
  resolution, because resolving `Nothing` on a legacy install yields `local` and bypasses
  `mode`. Consequence: in A an unpinned session *can* move when `mode` changes, where D9
  promises new sessions don't. **B restores D9 exactly** once `mode` is gone.
- **The general rule to check, not the individual cases:** *anywhere A converts a `Nothing`
  into a machine name, it can produce `local` and thereby bypass `mode`.* Four such places
  exist (read, signature, creation, inheritance). All are now absent or guarded.
- **Do not claim the hardened build.** Measured: `cabal build all -f remote-only-untrusted`
  **fails at link** and `test:tests` **does not compile** under the flag. So no DoD may
  require building or testing under it; the feature owes only the **runtime** coercion, and
  issue #347 owns the artifact property. **Recommend fixing #347 before B deletes `mode`.**
- **Issues #347 and #348 are out of scope.** #348 is `sanitizeHref` (frontend); B's WU-8 must
  not touch it.
